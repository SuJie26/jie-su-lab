Social-Ecological System Sustainability Lab

SESuS Lab

Our research applies social-ecological systems and ecological economics approaches to understand interactions between societies and ecosystems under global environmental change. We develop integrated approaches to coastal conservation and restoration that link biodiversity, climate mitigation and adaptation, economic development and social well-being.
